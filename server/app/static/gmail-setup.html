Send invites from your Gmail (Movinite 1.9)

Movinite can email invites from your own Gmail address, so the person you invite sees your name and your address, and replies come straight back to you. The easy way is a Gmail app password: about 2 minutes, once.

If you skip this, invites still work: Movinite gives you a link and a QR code to share yourself.

Gmail with an app password (recommended)

1. Turn on 2-Step Verification

App passwords only exist when 2-Step Verification is on.

  1. Open https://myaccount.google.com/security signed in as the Gmail account invites should come from.
  2. Under How you sign in to Google, open 2-Step Verification and turn it on (a phone prompt or an authenticator app is fine).

2. Create an app password

  1. Open https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.
  2. Type a name such as Movinite and press Create.
  3. Google shows a 16-letter password in four groups (like abcd efgh ijkl mnop). Copy it. You'll only see it once; if you lose it, delete it and make a new one.

Don't paste it into chat or email. It only goes into Movinite.

No "App passwords" page? It's hidden when 2-Step Verification is off, for some Google Workspace accounts where the admin turned it off, and for accounts using Advanced Protection. Use the advanced Connect Gmail option below, or another email provider.

3. Enter it in Movinite

  1. Movinite > Settings > Email > Send invites from your email.
  2. Send with: Gmail (app password).
  3. Gmail address: your full address, for example you@gmail.com.
  4. App password: the 16 letters (spaces are fine).
  5. From name: how you appear in the recipient's inbox. Use the name on your Google account so people know it's you. It's prefilled with the name Movinite knows for you.
  6. Press Save, then Send test email to myself. A sample invite arrives in your own inbox within a minute (check Spam the first time). The status changes to Working.

Movinite sends through smtp.gmail.com port 587 with STARTTLS. The From address is always your Gmail address and replies go to it. The app password is stored encrypted on the server (data/secrets/smtp.sealed, readable only by the Movinite service) and is never shown again or written to logs.

Send an invite

Settings > Users > Invite by email: their address, a role, the libraries, an optional note, and when the link expires. Press Send invite. It's also in your Gmail Sent folder.

Other email providers (SMTP)

Send with: Other email (SMTP server), then fill in what your provider documents:

Field Typical value
SMTP server smtp.example.com (Outlook.com: smtp-mail.outlook.com; iCloud: smtp.mail.me.com; Fastmail: smtp.fastmail.com)
Port / Security 587 + STARTTLS, or 465 + SSL/TLS
Username usually your full email address
Password your provider's app password where it has them
From address the address invites come from (most providers require it to be your own address)

A relay on your own network that doesn't need a sign-in can leave Username empty and use Security None.

Limits

  • Movinite sends at most 20 invites an hour and 100 a day through the account (change in Settings > Email). Test emails count too. Past the limit the invite is still created and you get the link to share yourself.
  • Gmail's own limit is about 500 messages a day for @gmail.com.

Turning it off

  • Settings > Email > Remove deletes the saved address and password from the server.
  • To stop the password working everywhere, delete it at https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords. Changing your Google password also revokes all app passwords.

Troubleshooting

Message Fix
"Gmail didn't accept the address and app password (535)" Check the address; make a new app password and save it again. Your normal Gmail password doesn't work here.
"Gmail needs an app password" (534) 2-Step Verification is off or a normal password was used. Turn it on and create an app password.
"temporarily refusing sign-ins" / "asked to try later" (421, 454) Too many tries or a burst of mail. Wait a few minutes.
"daily sending limit was reached" (550 5.4.5) Gmail's daily limit. Share links until tomorrow.
"Couldn't connect to smtp.gmail.com:587" The server can't reach the internet on port 587 (some ISPs and firewalls block mail ports). Try another network, or SSL/TLS on 465 under Other email.
"missing the Python 'cryptography' package" Re-run update.sh; it installs it.

Advanced: Connect Gmail with Google sign-in (OAuth)

Instead of an app password, Movinite can use Google sign-in with permission to send only (gmail.send); it can't read your mail and never sees your Google password. It takes about 10 minutes, once, in your own Google Cloud project. In Settings > Email open Advanced: Connect Gmail and choose Connect Gmail as the method. The From name is the name on your Google account.

1. Create a Google Cloud project

  1. Open https://console.cloud.google.com/ and sign in with the Google account you want invites to come from.
  2. Top bar > project picker > New project. Name it Movinite and press Create. Make sure it's selected.

2. Turn on the Gmail API

  1. Go to APIs & Services > Library (https://console.cloud.google.com/apis/library/gmail.googleapis.com).
  2. Open Gmail API and press Enable.

3. Set up the consent screen (Google Auth Platform)

  1. Go to APIs & Services > OAuth consent screen (newer consoles call it Google Auth Platform > Branding). Press Get started if asked.
  2. App name: Movinite. User support email: your address.
  3. Audience:
  4. Internal if you use Google Workspace and only people in your organisation will connect. No review needed.
  5. External for a normal @gmail.com account.
  6. Contact email: your address. Accept the policy and press Create.
  7. Data access (or Scopes) > Add or remove scopes. Add these four:
  8. openid
  9. .../auth/userinfo.email (email)
  10. .../auth/userinfo.profile (profile, used for the From name)
  11. https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send Save.
  12. External only, Audience page: either
  13. add yourself (and any other admin who will connect) under Test users, or
  14. press Publish app so its status is In production.

Why publish? While an External app is in Testing, Google expires its sign-ins after 7 days and Movinite will ask you to reconnect every week. In production keeps the connection until you remove it. Because Movinite asks for a sensitive scope, Google shows an "unverified app" warning for a personal project; it's your own app, so choose Advanced > Go to Movinite (unsafe). You don't need Google's verification for personal use (fewer than 100 users).

4. Create the OAuth client

  1. APIs & Services > Credentials > Create credentials > OAuth client ID (or Google Auth Platform > Clients > Create client).
  2. Application type: Web application. Name: Movinite.
  3. Authorized redirect URIs: add the exact addresses Movinite shows in Settings > Email. Usually:
  4. https://movies.example.com/api/gmail/callback — your public https address followed by /api/gmail/callback. Add one line for each public address you use (for example both media. and movies. names).
  5. http://localhost/movinite-gmail-callback — the paste address, for servers that only have a LAN or http address (Google only accepts https or localhost). Adding it is harmless even if you have an https address.
  6. Leave Authorized JavaScript origins empty. Press Create.
  7. Google shows the Client ID (ends in .apps.googleusercontent.com) and Client secret (starts with GOCSPX-). Keep this window open, or download the JSON. Treat the secret like a password: don't email it or put it in chat.

5. Paste them into Movinite

  1. Movinite > Settings > Email.
  2. (Optional) Public address: your https address, for example https://movies.example.com. Invite links and the redirect address use it.
  3. Client ID and Client secret: paste them and press Save. The secret is stored encrypted on the server (data/secrets/gmail.sealed, readable only by the Movinite service) and is never shown again.

6. Connect

  • Server with an https address: press Connect Gmail. Google's page opens; pick your account, leave Send email on your behalf ticked, and press Continue. You land back on Settings > Email showing "Connected as Your Name you@gmail.com".
  • LAN / http server (paste mode): press Connect Gmail (paste mode). After you approve on Google's page, the browser goes to http://localhost/movinite-gmail-callback?... and probably says This site can't be reached. That's expected. Copy the whole address from the address bar, paste it into the box in Settings > Email, and press Finish. The code inside it works once and only for a few minutes.

Press Send test email to send a sample invite to yourself (no real invite is created).

Limits

  • Movinite sends at most 20 invites an hour and 100 a day per connected account (change in Settings > Email). Past the limit the invite is still created and you get the link to share yourself.
  • Gmail's own limits apply too (about 500 messages a day for @gmail.com).

Disconnecting

  • Settings > Email > Disconnect revokes Movinite's access at Google and deletes the stored token.
  • You can also remove it from Google: https://myaccount.google.com/connections > Movinite > Delete all connections. Movinite then shows "Reconnect Gmail" and falls back to links.
  • Changing the client ID or secret disconnects every admin's Gmail (their sign-ins belong to the old client).

Troubleshooting

Message Fix
redirect_uri_mismatch (Google error page) The redirect URI in Google Cloud must match the one in Settings > Email exactly: https, host, no trailing slash. Changes in Google Cloud can take a few minutes.
"Access blocked: Movinite has not completed the Google verification process" External app in Testing and your account isn't a test user. Add it under Audience > Test users, or publish the app.
"Google didn't return a long-lived sign-in" Remove Movinite at https://myaccount.google.com/connections and connect again.
Reconnect every 7 days The app is still in Testing. Publish it (In production).
"missing the Python 'cryptography' package" Re-run update.sh; it installs it.
